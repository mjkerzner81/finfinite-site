A fish-eat-fish adventure

Finfinite is a pixel-art game for iPhone. Start as a tiny fish, eat anything smaller than you, and steer clear of anything bigger. Grow through 100 levels across ten zones of ocean, from sunny shallows to the abyss, and take on a boss at the end of each one.

Along the way: rare visitors to catch, a rival barracuda to settle the score with, bonus stages, holiday events, a daily challenge, an endless mode, and a tank to fill with everything you've found.

No ads. No accounts. Every pixel, note and gulp is made in code.
