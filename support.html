Contact

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Email:

finfinite.support@gmail.com

It helps to include what happened, which level you were on, your iPhone model, and the version number shown at the bottom of the title screen. A screenshot is always welcome.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen to swim. Eat fish smaller than you to grow, and swim away from anything bigger. Fill your growth bar three times to clear a level. Tap the HOW TO button on the title screen for the full guide.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you're signed in. That means a new phone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account picks up where you left off, even after deleting the app. The full-game unlock comes back too: tap RESTORE on the About screen if it doesn't appear straight away.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Tap the bell next to the music and sound buttons on the title screen. When it's on, Finfinite sends one nudge at 6 PM on days your fish are hungry or a new daily challenge is waiting. It's made on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us.

Does Finfinite have ads?

No. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Something looks wrong or the game froze

Close Finfinite completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. Your progress is saved as you play. If it keeps happening, email us with the details above.
