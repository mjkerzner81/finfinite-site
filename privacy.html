The short version

Finfinite does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Effective October 26, 2026 (updated to describe iCloud saves). Finfinite is made by Michael J Kerzner ("we" below).

What we collect

Nothing. Finfinite has no sign-up, no analytics or advertising tools, and does not send any information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars, pearls and unlocks are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud storage, so your progress follows you to a new phone or survives deleting the app. Only your devices can read it; we can't see it. You can turn this off under Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Sharing pictures

Finfinite can make a picture of a big moment or of your tank. It is only shared if you tap share and pick where it goes, using your iPhone's own share sheet. We don't receive a copy.

Apple services

The full-game purchase, iCloud saves and Game Center leaderboards are provided by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

Children

Finfinite is a general-audience game. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect in the app.
